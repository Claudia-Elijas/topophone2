**What is a topophone?**

A topophone is a device that converts sound into electrical signals. It is used in various applications, such as telecommunication, audio recording, and sound analysis. 
The topophone2 package provides tools for processing and analyzing sound data, making it easier for researchers and engineers to work with acoustic signals.
